## Interface language

Spanish (default) and English, with a selector in the top bar. Dictionary in `frontend/lib/i18n-es.ts`; English text is the key and missing keys fall back to English. Free text generated by the backend (rejection reasons, model `reason`, Markdown reports) stays in English.

## Plain-language summary and tutorial

- **Overview → "What is happening, in plain words"** (`frontend/lib/explain.ts`): explains the state of the lab for non-experts. It is written by **fixed rules from the live API numbers, not by a language model**, which keeps the no-LLM constraint and means it cannot invent figures. It covers money, evidence, best and worst wallet, idle arbitrage, AI models, open bets, drawdown and feed status.
- **Guided tutorial** (`frontend/components/tour/tour.tsx`): 15 steps across 7 pages. It spotlights the element being explained, and a magnifier lens follows the mouse over it. Use ←/→ or the buttons, Esc to close. It starts automatically on the first visit, and the graduation-cap button in the top bar restarts it. Steps whose target is not on screen (for example the sidebar on mobile) show a centred card.

## Phase 2: profitability validation (2026-10-28)

Done and verified:
- **Audit** (docs/AUDIT_PHASE2.md): the arithmetic is correct and reconciles to the cent. The profits were **not** statistically meaningful: 15 independent trades, and the CI includes zero. Critical findings were fixed: stale book levels, the lax freshness limit, AI longshot bets, double-counted totals and i.i.d. statistics.
- **Execution Validator** (`analytics/validation.py`): fills against the worse of our book and the exchange's REST book; real-trade corroboration via the Data API; lifecycle funnel; per-order trace. The first 20 historic momentum fills were corroborated by real trades.
- **Scenarios** OPTIMISTIC / REALISTIC / CONSERVATIVE, editable in Settings. CONSERVATIVE adds a safety margin. BASE was renamed REALISTIC by migration 0002, without rewriting history.
- **Verifiable accounting** (`portfolio.reconcile`): available, reserved (always 0: fill-and-kill orders), cost basis, realised and unrealised PnL, equity and net return. Rebuilt via two independent routes, with a shares-conservation check that catches double settlement.
- **Profitability Analyzer** (`analytics/profitability.py`): per (wallet, strategy). Gross and net PnL, ROI on deployed capital, win rate, profit factor, expectancy, average win and loss, drawdown, exposure, average capital, duration, execution ratio, rejection reasons, fee sensitivity, three-scenario re-pricing, market-implied baseline, cluster bootstrap CI, stability halves. No annualising under 30 days.
- **Gates** (docs/PROFITABILITY_GATES.md), with status changes logged as `gate` events.
- **Profitability Center** page (strategy table, per-strategy report with charts and trades, order traces, execution models, real-exchange checks, funnel, gates, research ranking). Unproven gains are never shown in green.
- **Persisted wallet decisions**, a **kill switch**, the **venue interface (design only)** and the **research ranking** (docs/OPPORTUNITY_RESEARCH.md).
- Tests: 45 passing. New ones cover the validator, cluster statistics, profit-before-costs-but-loss-after, gates, configurable scenarios, freshness and crossed books, AI caps, reconciliation tampering, a vanished opportunity, the exchange-book partial fill, and backend↔frontend consistency.

## Results so far (≈1 hour of data; NOT evidence)

- ML: on 1,085 out-of-sample markets, the market price beats both models on log loss (logistic −0.02, t≈−2; LightGBM −0.07, t≈−5). The naive betting rule shows positive ROI on historical prices, but those are last-trade/mid prices, not executable asks, so this is unconfirmed until forward tests say otherwise. The models do not trade.
- Arbitrage: no executable opportunity seen.
- Crypto momentum: a handful of settled trades with mixed results. Far too few to say anything (n=3 ≪ 30).

## Experimental AI mode (since 2026-10-28)

At the user's request, `ai_lightgbm` and `ai_logistic` run with `require_validation = 0`. They trade even though both models failed the out-of-sample gate, as a live test of whether the gate was right.
Those signals carry `unvalidated_model: true` and risk "very high", and the plain-language summary says so. Set `require_validation` back to 1 in Strategies to restore the gate.
Mean reversion now triggers on a 6-point move in 60 min (was 10 in 30), and 40 markets are monitored.
These parameters were changed to get more activity, not to fit past PnL.

## Known issues and limitations

- **Not yet run for several days.** Only ~1 h of continuous operation has been verified; the multi-day run (success criterion 13) is pending.
- 5m/15m crypto markets resolve on Chainlink TWAP, and Binance is only a proxy. The model includes a 3 bps basis term and a `max_edge` guard, but model error remains the most likely source of any "edge".
- The daily loss limit blocks new positions only. Positions already open can lose more than the limit (at most 5 × 5 USDC by default).
- The `book` feature set (spread, imbalance, depth) only trains once enough markets this lab recorded have resolved. Until then it shows INSUFFICIENT TRAINING DATA.
- Backtest books contain top-of-book only (deeper levels are not stored), and ML strategies are not replayed: they are evaluated by walk-forward and live scoring instead.
- On 2026‑10‑28, ~1,000 WS messages/s at 20 markets cost ~12 % of one core. At 50 markets, expect roughly double. A "slow consumer" disconnect was observed once at startup and recovered automatically.
- The Settings "Notifications" toggle is stored but does not yet silence toasts.
- XGBoost is supported but not installed by default (image size).
- The PostgreSQL migration path is designed (`Dec` → `Numeric`) but untested.
- Dev mode only: next-themes triggers a harmless "script tag" console warning.
